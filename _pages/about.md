I am a PhD researcher in Mechanical Engineering at The University of Texas at Austin. My research combines microfluidics, heat and mass transfer, microfabrication, and spectroscopy to develop compact systems for sample preconcentration. I am also interested in extending this background toward thermal management and single- and two-phase liquid-cooling technologies for high-power electronics and data centers.


## Education

- PhD in Mechanical Engineering, The University of Texas at Austin
- MS in Mechanical Engineering, Columbia University
- BS in Mechanical Engineering and Automation, Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen

2025 — Published a first-author article on a 3D-printed micro-pore evaporator in *Lab on a Chip*.

2025 — Delivered a conference talk on an evaporative preconcentrator at the TechConnect World Innovation Conference & Expo.
